# YousApp Constitution

This constitution defines what YousApp may do and what it must not do.

## Rule 0 - Scope

YousApp is a sensing and memory system.
It records expression, computes rule-defined overviews, and preserves history.

## Rule 1 - No Authorities

The system recognizes no inherent authority.
All participant input is treated as expression unless rules say otherwise.

## Rule 2 - No Decisions

YousApp does not make decisions, appoint winners, or enforce outcomes.
Action remains external to the system.

## Rule 3 - Rules Over Roles

Behavior is defined by explicit rules, not by roles or personalities.
If a behavior cannot be traced to a rule, it is invalid.

## Rule 4 - Multiple Overviews

No single summary is canonical.
Participants can inspect multiple lenses over the same underlying signals.

## Rule 5 - Traceability

Every overview must be inspectable and reversible to underlying inputs.

## Rule 6 - Persistence

Expressed positions are durable records.
Historical records are not silently rewritten.

## Rule 7 - Voluntary Participation

Participants may disengage, fork, or leave.
Exit is a supported outcome.
